# KHP-OS Intelligence Layer v2

## Purpose

KHP-OS Intelligence Layer v2 deepens the existing transformation lifecycle without expanding KHP-OS into an ERP. The deterministic KHP-OS methodology remains authoritative for diagnosis, priority eligibility, canonical intervention selection, review rules and human decision rights. AI is used only to interpret evidence and contextualise execution.

The product rule remains: every capability must materially support the discovery, development or deployment of human potential, or provide evidence that one of those outcomes is improving.

## Intelligence flow

`KSHC evidence → deterministic priority → canonical intervention → institutional context → AI contextualisation → outcome contract → implementation → evidence → deterministic review recommendation + AI explanation → human decision → reassessment`

## What v2 adds

1. **Transformation Context** — a versioned evidence snapshot assembled from the institution identity, KSHC baseline, 55 indicators, report intelligence and active transformation agenda.
2. **Intervention Intelligence** — the selected KAEC-NG intervention is retained, but its meaning, problem interpretation, urgency, actions, milestones, evidence requirements and guardrails become institution-specific.
3. **Outcome Contracts** — every AI-contextualised intervention states the baseline condition, desired condition, leading indicators, outcome indicators, success threshold and evidence standard. Activity completion is not treated as transformation.
4. **Contextual Implementation** — a validated AI-assisted plan replaces the untouched generic system plan only before execution begins. Once execution has started, KHP-OS refuses to rewrite history.
5. **Adaptive Review** — deterministic review recommendations remain fixed by evidence rules; AI explains what changed, what remains unproven, whether the main issue appears to be execution or intervention fit, what evidence is missing and what adaptation deserves consideration.
6. **Provenance** — AI source, model, generation version and timestamps are persisted throughout intervention, implementation, outcome-contract and review intelligence.

## Safety boundaries

- AI never changes KSHC scores.
- AI never approves a priority or replaces the canonical intervention selected by KHP-OS.
- AI never marks a priority resolved.
- Implementation evidence is not represented as verified institutional improvement.
- Reassessment remains the valid mechanism for verifying movement in the diagnosed institutional condition.
- Human Executives and Transformation Leads retain consequential decision authority.
- If AI is unavailable or fails validation, the existing deterministic plan remains usable and the fallback is recorded rather than hidden.

## Existing cycles

Untouched system-generated plans may be upgraded lazily when an authorised Executive or Transformation Lead opens Implementation. The upgrade is allowed only if execution has not started, no evidence has been submitted and no review snapshot exists. This makes the upgrade safe for current partners without requiring a new KSHC assessment.
