# Attendance location evidence

Teachers use School Attendance & HQLS → School attendance. Authorised Custodians and campus Guardians open **Campus attendance locations**, select a campus, enter its known latitude/longitude or use the current device position while on site, choose a 50–1,000 metre boundary and explain the setup. HQ Vision Custodian or Alliance Catalyst can configure the same campus pins from an active school's Pathfinder panel. School Pathfinders and System Stewards can read campus pins in their supervision scope but cannot change them.

On-site Pathfinder schedules select an active campus belonging to that school. Previously scheduled visits without a campus select it at arrival. Virtual activities do not request GPS. A visit's campus cannot change after arrival.

Each school or on-site visit check-in/out requests one fresh device position (12 second browser timeout; 15 second fallback). The screen explains purpose, supervisor access and no background tracking before the action. Denied, unavailable, unsupported or timed-out capture opens a reason dialog; the user can record with a 5–500 character explanation or cancel. An optional attendance explanation supports off-site duties. Existing clients can still record attendance; missing location is explicitly marked not recorded rather than silently passing verification.

The server validates coordinate/accuracy ranges, records its receipt time, snapshots the campus boundary, and calculates distance using a clamped Haversine calculation. A reading older than two minutes or over 30 seconds in the future is flagged stale. Accuracy worse than 100 m is flagged low accuracy. A reading wholly inside the boundary including its accuracy circle is within boundary; wholly outside is outside; intersecting the boundary needs accuracy review. No configured pin is a separate flag. Attendance is never rejected merely because GPS is unavailable, inaccurate or off-site.

Location evidence is saved atomically with the teacher attendance action. Pathfinder updates include timestamp, location and lifecycle change in the existing optimistic update; a stale update does not save partial evidence. Pin edits are version checked and audited; existing arrival/departure evidence retains its original pin snapshot. Historical records remain null and are displayed as no location evidence. Administrative closure does not invent a departure coordinate.

Teacher evidence follows the existing school attendance history permission: Custodian, Guardian or Academic Inspector within the permitted campus scope, plus the staff member's own current attendance. No HQ role gains access to teacher GPS through this change. Pathfinder evidence follows the existing corporate session scope and current assigned representative; unrelated schools/Pathfinders and other corporate roles cannot access it. Campus configuration and evaluation RPCs are service-role-only; there are no new client table grants. External maps open only if a reader chooses **View location on map**.

Location supports review, not automatic proof of presence or work. Device coordinates can be spoofed, and the independent outcome review remains necessary. Exception filters cover the currently loaded history page or the capped Pathfinder session list, as labelled; they are not an automated escalation or push notification system.

Privacy policy includes point-in-time coordinates, accuracy, purpose, viewers and retention alongside attendance. Before collecting this new location data through the Play-distributed app, the owner must update its Play Console Data safety declaration to reflect precise location collection for attendance/app functionality and ensure school staff are informed. No background permission, paid map service or Android binary change is introduced. Physical phone/browser permission behavior must still be checked on the installed app.

Verification: `node scripts/test-attendance-location-sql.mjs` executes real migration/functions in PGlite with allow/deny and boundary cases; `node scripts/test-attendance-location-browser.mjs` executes the browser capture code with controlled geolocation success/failure/timeout paths. Full repository CI runs both.
