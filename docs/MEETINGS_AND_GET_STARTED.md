# Independent HQ meetings and simpler entry

KAEC HQ → Meetings now opens the corporate meeting workspace directly. Corporate rooms have no school organisation reference. Every active HQ member, including every appointed Leadership Council member, can host and invite active HQ colleagues and leaders of active licensed schools. Explicit invitations are still required for reading, messages and calling. Being a host elsewhere never grants access to a private room.

School meeting authority is unchanged. Corporate meeting follow-ups can be assigned to active invited HQ colleagues and create existing HQ Work meeting_action records atomically. Cross-school invitees can discuss and join calls; assigning them school execution tasks from a corporate room is not included. HQ invitees use their existing Work review/evidence controls. School leaders also see their invited corporate calls through school Today and notifications. The invite picker supports name/email search and shows selection count.

Getting started now offers four small views: Your position, Read policies, Try the basics, Finish setup. The next required step is selected automatically, and only the current practice question is shown. Role/process reference content is not forced into the initial view. Existing acknowledgement, evidence, approval and activation rules are unchanged; the UI does not automatically waive requirements or grant access.

Existing school staff see Continue your work with Today and Get started before diagnostic/assessment links on the account page. Staff without a linked school are directed to ask their school to add the same sign-in email rather than being told to create a school partnership.

Validation covers standalone HQ rooms, explicit school leader invitations, uninvited HQ and school denial, suspended-user denial, transactional HQ action creation, retry safety and existing school isolation. Mobile onboarding and real calling remain device-dependent checks.

## Invitation directory

HQ invitations have separate HQ-colleague and school-leader groups. Choose a school by name before its leaders appear. Selections persist across schools/groups, with one invitation per person. School-hosted meetings show that school’s staff separately from HQ colleagues. Every entry and the invited-person list shows the saved name and account email. Hosts receive school affiliations only for eligible active members; membership, leadership and private-room checks remain server-enforced.
